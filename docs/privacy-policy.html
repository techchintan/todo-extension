QuickTask — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026
Extension: QuickTask (Chrome)

QuickTask helps you save todos and receive Chrome notifications when items start or are due. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how it is used.

Data we handle

The extension stores the information you enter or save as todos, including:

  • Todo titles, descriptions/notes, tags, and priorities
  • Start and due dates/times, completion status, and recurrence settings
  • Page titles and URLs when you explicitly save the current page or selection as a todo
  • Settings such as whether Chrome sync is enabled

Selected text is only saved when you choose “Add selection as todo” (or an equivalent action). The extension does not browse or scrape websites in the background.

How data is stored

  • By default, todos and settings are stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local.
  • If you enable Chrome sync in Settings, the same data may be stored in chrome.storage.sync and synchronized through your Google account according to Google’s sync and storage limits.

QuickTask does not operate its own servers and does not upload your todos to a developer-controlled backend.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No sale of user data
  • No remote code loading from third-party sites
  • No automatic collection of browsing history beyond titles/URLs you explicitly save as todos

Permissions (why they exist)

  • storage — save todos and settings
  • alarms — schedule start/due reminders
  • notifications — show start/due alerts and a Complete action
  • contextMenus — add the current page or selection as a todo from the right-click menu
  • activeTab — read the current tab title/URL only when you invoke the extension (for example, Save current page in the popup)

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your todos with third parties. If Chrome sync is enabled, Google may process synced storage as part of Chrome Sync under Google’s own policies.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual todos or clear completed items in the extension
  • Turn off Chrome sync in Settings
  • Uninstall the extension to remove local extension storage
  • Use Export / Import in the popup if you want a personal backup copy

Children

QuickTask is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the extension’s data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when that happens.
